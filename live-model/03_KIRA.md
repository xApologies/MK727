# Kira — MK727 working story baseline
Opening age **23**, Afflicted (chalk/calcified white skin, surface growths, brittle white hair, steel-gray eyes), expected Afflicted lifespan approximately 11–17, not a hard cutoff. The alleged oldest 37-year survivor is unverified. She is positive and resourceful despite exploitation.

Mother was an apothecary; father an absent passing aithren. Mother died rapidly of a Genesis-related illness when Kira was eight (disease mechanism OPEN). A transactional blacksmith took her in for cheap labor. She sleeps near the forge, wears threadbare clothes, receives minimal food and roughly one Red coin daily. At 23 she has ~15 years of expert **manual** chainmail craft (wire drawing, mandrel, ring cutting, closures, weaving, repairs), but owns **no cards**. Smith holds Crown guard armor repair contract; Kira makes village-hub deliveries and gathers flowers, mushrooms, moss and occasional Genesis materials for under-market apothecary payments. She saves almost everything for Affliction treatment.

**Restoration potion:** known recipe, expensive because six intact cut Genesis crystals R–V are required at six anchors in a closed sigil; dust traces selected ru’ne channels. A vial of prepared conventional ingredients is transduced as crystals are consumed into a **White-superposed liquid lattice**. This is NOT a White Genesis crystal (different material identity and lattice; White crystals naturally form and cannot currently be manufactured). Typical treated Afflicted become healthy, retaining steel-gray eyes. Kira's treatment instead resolves into extraordinary Genesis-integrated biology inherited as phenotype inspiration from MK157: healthy multilayered white skin, full white hair, black eyes with R–V depth, refined symmetry, lithe dense build, ~5'11, Genesis-integrated skeletal/neural structures. Do NOT import MK157's Valnak/Elara/Black Binding transformation mechanism, relationships or plot. Potion price/recipe, future card acquisition and Kira story arcs OPEN.
