# Kira — MK727 revised biography (supersedes First Light)

**ESTABLISHED:** Kira opens at age 23, Afflicted, introverted, resourceful, curious and generally positive despite exploitation. Typical Afflicted lifespan ~11–17, not a universal cutoff; oldest 37 claim unverified. Pre-restoration phenotype: chalky/calcified white skin, growths, brittle white hair, steel-gray eyes.

## Ages 0–15 — her mother, a Cardsmith
Her mother is a licensed Cardsmith (card engineer), not an apothecary; M05 distinguishes this duty from Architect/Runesmith sigil design. Her absent father was a passing `aithren`. Mother and daughter lived frugally but adequately in a modest regional workshop, saving toward Kira's costly Affliction restoration. Her mother taught Genesis Field fundamentals, mathematics, sigils, material identity, and card construction. Kira is geometrically intuitive, not automatically a prodigy in formal mathematics. Kira keeps her mother's **three books** (Genesis Field, Genesis mathematics, ru’ne/sigils), ordinary professional references and her only lasting possessions of sentimental importance. Exact book titles OPEN.

At age **15**, mother develops a rapidly fatal illness. They buy an expensive alchemical treatment; it fails. Specialized medical expertise is not readily accessible in the developing region. The treatment and Kira's subsequent subsistence consume most remaining restoration savings. Kira attempts to maintain the Cardsmith workshop but loses customers due to Afflicted stigma; the workshop's final property disposition OPEN.

## Ages 16–23 — the armorsmith
At **16**, a Crown-contracted armorsmith employs her at the minimum legally defensible wage, deducting room/board for sleeping by the forge and one evening meal (which she often splits with breakfast). She is effectively economically coerced; her threadbare clothes are repeatedly mended. She spends **seven years** manually drawing wire, winding mandrels, cutting, closing and weaving rings, repairing mail and performing deliveries. At 23 her chainmail skill is exceptional, but she has not mastered Genesis-assisted metal manipulation. The smith owns a valuable donkey and cart used for deliveries to guard posts, villages and Hollowmere dungeon guards and adventuring support. Kira collects flora, fungi, moss, occasional Genesis materials and sells them cheaply to apothecaries to rebuild a small restoration fund.

**Starting equipment:** one ordinary Fireball Genesis Card (WORKING; verify actual catalogue ID/basin assignment before prose). She can operate it, but her Afflicted endurance allows only approximately two or three shots in the collapse confrontation before fatigue. She does not have a large collection of active cards. Her mother's three books remain her most valued possessions.

## Collapse, healing, restoration
On delivery Kira becomes the final destabilizing condition for a pre-existing local Genesis-collapse instability (WORKING event cause). She survives Eldris, severe injuries, and harvests R–V crystals plus exceptionally rare **White crystal**; **NO Black crystal**. At a guard outpost, a recently arrived alchemist who sells healing potions treats her with an **ordinary MK117-style healing potion**. It accelerates normal repair and leaves significant facial and back scars; it does not cure Affliction.

A standard Affliction restoration potion requires six intact, appropriately cut R–V crystals, dust-reinforced ru’ne channels, a closed sigil, and prepared material ingredients in a vial. The crystals are consumed in Transduction; the finished **White-superposed liquid lattice** is **not** a White Genesis crystal. Ordinary restoration produces stable human biology, retaining steel-gray eyes. Kira's alchemist also consumes the rare **White crystal** as an extraordinary stabilizing catalyst to destroy dangerously valuable evidence. This is not a standard recipe ingredient. Kira's abnormal Genesis biology resolves into the MK157-inspired phenotype: healthy multilayered white skin, fuller white hair, black eyes with R–V spectral depth, refined symmetry, lithe dense build (~5'11), Genesis-integrated skeleton/neural structures. Existing healed scars remain. Her transformation opens unified Black/Neon access; no Valnak/Elara/Black Binding causality is inherited.

Leftover cut chromatic shards constitute modest but real wealth, enough to escape the smithy, leave the village-hub region and establish herself in a city. She is not made limitlessly wealthy. Her future includes chainmail couture, Cardsmithing, Blackstone and `aithren` exploration.

**SUPERSEDED:** mother apothecary; death at 8; 15 years in smithy; starting with zero cards; MK117 Black crystal; MK157 Valnak mechanism.
