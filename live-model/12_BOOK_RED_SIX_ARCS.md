# Book Red — Six-Arc Narrative Spine
**Status:** ESTABLISHED arc count and sequence; detailed scenes and chronology OPEN. Arc-first development; chapters serve arcs rather than define them.

| Arc | Name | Story function | Endpoint / dependency |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | Village Hub | Kira/Kaira's exploitative armorsmith life, delivery, collapse, emergency healing, White-catalyzed Affliction restoration | Health and economic independence; leave the hub |
| II | Orange District | New city life, relationships, independent armorsmith/chainmail work, first robe development | First robe and nascent independent business |
| III | Chainmail Robe Movement | Robe popularity, commissions, independent income, cultural distinction from ranked maegi robes | Sustainable craft/business identity, path to Blackstone |
| IV | Blackstone | Higher education in Genesis mathematics, topology, sigils, material science and Cardsmithing; first Black Armor card | Armor of the Abyss; academic competence does not equal combat competence |
| V | Aithren Community | Kaira joins **to adventure**, develops dungeon-delving skill, relationships, combat competency, explores and collects resources | Competent enough to enter the King's Tournament |
| VI | King's Tournament | Inherited MK147 dungeon race and competition | **Book Red ends with Kaira receiving a Dimensional Ring as her tournament reward** |

**Tournament inheritance:** MK147 persistent dungeon racing: bottom → token → surface; navigation, avoidance and logistics; qualification → regional circuit → top six → kingdom finals; source points V6/B5/G4/Y3/O2/R1. “Purple Region” is OPEN in MK147; do not resolve by invention. Exact result, placing, ring quality, event choreography and qualifying details remain OPEN.

**Book Red equipment continuity:** Kaira has NO dimensional ring during Arcs I–V **or during Arc VI's competition**. She must carry a conventional expedition pack and its burdens. The ring is awarded at the end. Books Orange–White can treat it as standard equipment, simplifying carrying tools, provisions, clothing, cards, crafting supplies and mother's books; preparation and conscious retrieval remain relevant.

**Chronology:** No fixed chapter counts, travel dates or duration invented. Original MK117 seven-arc Book One is *source scaffolding*, not MK727's current six-arc count. The MK117 healer/hospital career is not transferred.
