# Realms and starscape — Milestone 04

**Status: AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED MK727 realization, not a universal AERA requirement.** Source: [M04 realm decisions](../provenance/milestone-04/decisions/05_REALMS.md). This clarifies and expands the existing [world and sprite summary](02_WORLD.md).

## Structural basins and chosen inhabited realms

AERA requires six R–V energy structures and White superposition. It permits authors to realize them as energy basins alone, inhabited/traversable realms, or other compatible implementations. MK147 may use basins without adopting inhabited realms. **MK727 chooses six inhabitable chromatic realms plus the White material realm.**

MK727 adopts approximately **80% dominant basin plus 20% scaffold** from the other five for its chromatic realms. The residual proportions need not be equal. White is balanced six-basin superposition; approximately one sixth each is an author illustration, not a universal equilibrium formula. The [configuration screenshot](../references/m04-visuals/README.md#visual-register) is supporting visual provenance, not a mandatory AERA chart.

Colors do not mean fire/water/nature elements, monochrome scenery, professions or power ranks. Book colors are narrative labels and impose neither a matching realm itinerary nor a fixed realm-visit sequence. Existing Red dragon and Blue leviathan assignments remain WORKING.

## Geometry and visual limits

Realms can have non-ordinary topology and differential geometry, multiple locally oriented traversable landscapes, a “sky” that is another ground/ocean, connecting mountains and changing local up/down. The overhead-ocean waterfall image is a **VISUAL REFERENCE**, not a universal realm shape or a world map.

Locally changing effective gravity does not prove nonorientability. Exact physics, metric, hydrology, transport and landscape arrangement remain OPEN; no equations, river circulation mechanism or gateways are invented from an image.

## Native compatibility and passage

The author establishes the following ordinary compatibility rule for MK727:

| Native origin | Ordinary inhabitable environments |
| --- | --- |
| White-native humans/denizens | White and all six chromatic realms |
| Red | Red and White |
| Orange | Orange and White |
| Yellow | Yellow and White |
| Green | Green and White |
| Blue | Blue and White |
| Violet | Violet and White |

Compatibility is permission to exist, not automatic access or travel. Gateways, route mechanics, passage coordinates and exceptions are OPEN. A dungeon or domai is not automatically a realm portal. Rainbow Road remains inherited transport groundwork, not a completed MK727 route map.

## Existing sprites and open biology

Sprites already have a generalized MK727 description: small playful visibly chromatic Genesis energy entities. A collective/hive may assume forms, potentially a humanlike form for White travel. This preserves the existing world summary rather than claiming no sprites have been defined.

Exact ontology, embodiment, lifespan, population, limits and mechanisms are OPEN. A possible collective form does not establish an unverified species rule, automatic individual shapeshifting, or access to otherwise incompatible realms. Yellow living forest remains ILLUSTRATIVE.

## Interworld bridges and story use

MK727 adopts chromatic realms as traversable interworld bridge environments. Multiple White worlds, potentially planets in other galaxies, may access the same realms. A human traveler can enter from one White world and eventually reach another; actual passage mechanics, coordinates and duration remain OPEN. No universal local clock ratios or instant intergalactic transport are implied.

Kaira's late Orange/early Yellow and later exploration can occupy major portions of books, driven by vaen research, encounters, profession and independent exploration. This is a flexible story direction, not a compulsory departure date, route or realm sequence. M05 reports that the author has accepted an illustrated world atlas derived from original geography/climate maps; its bytes have not been supplied to this repository. Exact map georeferencing remains OPEN.
