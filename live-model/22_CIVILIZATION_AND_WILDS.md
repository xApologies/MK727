# Civilization, nobles and the Wilds — Milestone 05

Source: [M05 civilization decisions](../provenance/milestone-05/decisions/02_CIVILIZATION.md). **ESTABLISHED author direction** unless marked WORKING, ILLUSTRATIVE or OPEN below. Compatible source inheritance is recorded in the [M05 source register](../provenance/MILESTONE_05_SOURCE_REGISTER.md). These are MK727 choices, not new universal AERA laws.

## Stabilized islands and roads

Civilization occupies stabilized islands within a vast Genesis-risk Wilds landscape. **Wilds is a risk condition, not a biome**: storms, collapse, Eldris, altered flora/fauna and rare vaen can occur across ecosystems. Stabilization reduces risk without making a place immune to all environmental or human dangers.

Roads and guard waystations connect protected settlements. Guard detachments patrol routes, often for months per circuit and years per assignment; these are general durations, not a new scene calendar. Waystations support lodging, repairs and resupply. Organic towns can develop through traffic and trade, but are not automatically Crown Village Hubs. MK727's opening hub remains a **planned Village Hub whose economy centers on the Hollowmere dungeon**; source descriptions of organic dungeon-support towns do not change its civic classification.

## Districts and landed shares

Small-city districts run **Red outer → Orange middle → Yellow inner → White civic center**. Three chromatic landed nobles own their corresponding districts and surrounding stabilized rural holdings. Rural land allocation is Red **1/6**, Orange **2/6**, Yellow **3/6**.

Large-city and capital districts run **Red → Orange → Yellow → Green → Blue → Violet inward, then White center**. Six chromatic landed nobles own their corresponding districts and rural territory in **1:2:3:4:5:6** shares, denominator **21**.

| Chromatic noble | Small-city rural share | Large-city/capital rural share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Red | 1/6 | 1/21 |
| Orange | 2/6 | 2/21 |
| Yellow | 3/6 | 3/21 |
| Green | Not a small-city district | 4/21 |
| Blue | Not a small-city district | 5/21 |
| Violet | Not a small-city district | 6/21 |

Rural shares **exclude urban area**. They describe allocation, not exact geometric wedges. Terrain, overlap and open water matter; no acreage, boundary coordinates or proportional urban district sizes are inferred.

## Civic responsibilities

Chromatic nobles manage district sanitation, safety, infrastructure and property taxes. Their landed holdings can include estates, orchards, vineyards and leased alchemical farmland. The **White noble is a Crown-appointed civic overseer**, supervising district nobles with **no separate landed rural share**.

The Crown may replace neglectful White administrators. Severe negligence by chromatic nobles can lead to sanctions or loss of nobility. Red districts are affordable and dense, not inherently dirty or crime-ridden. Civic duty means maintaining security, not guaranteeing literal zero crime. Tax rates and succession remain OPEN.

## Milestone 07 Afflicted social continuity

**ESTABLISHED author direction:** Afflicted people remain beneath the conventional **noble/commoner/peasant hierarchy**. Housing, employment, pricing and social opportunities remain restricted. Civic security, infrastructure and the relative affordability of a district do not establish equal opportunity or remove this social exclusion.

The Village Hub is close-knit, rumor-driven and overtly superstitious, including **Eternal Ladder gestures and direct harassment**. Urban people are generally more educated about Affliction being a **noncontagious genetic condition**, yet taboo persists. Common treatment shifts toward indifference, dismissal, exclusion and commercial inflexibility. Individuals vary; neither pattern describes every person.

Kaira's physical restoration leaves her visibly white-skinned and perceived as Afflicted; her class standing does not rise when she moves to the city. [The apartment negotiation](21_M04_BOOK_RED_EXPANSION.md#milestone-07-arc-ii-apartment-opening) illustrates this continued exclusion without establishing a rental policy, price schedule or uniform surcharge. The earlier Chapter One phrase "Afflicted commoner" records the hostile noble son's perception, not equality within the ordinary commoner tier; M07 explicitly places Afflicted people beneath the conventional hierarchy.

## Milestone 07 ordinary city architecture

**INHERITED / ESTABLISHED descriptive standard:** Compatible MK147 construction and [M06 phase-grown stone](25_CONTINENTAL_POLITIES_AND_TRADE.md#stone-joinery-and-employment) support ordinary **pale stone**: ivory tones, limestone, marble and sandstone. Buildings are orthogonal **square, rectangular or L-shaped structures**, with **flat roofs** and usually **2–4 occupied stories**. Recurring forms include terraces, arcades, planted courtyards, open plazas and fountains. Restrained canals/waterways apply **where mapped**. Blackstone's specialized dark stone is not the ordinary default; the usual story count is not a universal height cap.

The [six author-supplied architecture references](../references/m07-architecture/README.md) illustrate selected materials, building forms, greenery and public spaces. They are **architecture only**, not geographic/cartographic authority or new plot/spatial facts. Existing pixel maps remain authoritative for routes and locations. Zoning colors are **functional identifiers, not roof colors**. Colorful pitched roofs, generic medieval-fantasy substitutions, invented moats, symmetrical palace layouts and relocated roads are rejected as canon.

No image's incidental skyline, water, mountain, road or roof detail establishes a mapped location or overrides this written standard. Failed rendering trials, Procreate-layer screenshots, drawing instructions, procedural cartography recommendations and new map assets are excluded from this checkpoint. The author will provide maps when needed for actual scenes. This adds descriptive continuity without assigning the Arc II city, apartment or geography.
