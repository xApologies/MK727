## Access and projection

The orthogonal sigil workbench is an **access/projection workspace**, not an automatic editor. A physical maege-glass card anchors its embedded Genesis manifold. Activating the sigil exposes an isolated phase-state shadow projection of that topology in an accessible working field. The projection remains coupled to the underlying card; it is not an independent copy. Deactivating the sigil removes access/projection, and the physical card remains a card. Workbench on/off is not a separate editing action.

The workbench sigil is architect-made; professional Genesis Cards operated by a Cardsmith supply observation and manipulation of the exposed field. The operator supplies mathematics, competency and judgment. Incorrect changes can break topology and spider-fracture or ruin the maege glass. The workstation does not replace skill or protect every experiment from failure.

## Modification and original construction

Modifying an existing card changes its embedded manifold. Original engineering begins with **blank maege glass**, which has no embedded Shaping. Its detailed initialization/embedding mechanism still requires specification. The earlier [manufacturing summary](01_MAGIC.md) establishes industrial sigil flashing and ingredient-consuming collapse at a high level; it is not a complete original Black Card recipe.

Kaira begins with her mother's three books and childhood instruction, not a full qualification. Arc III can include experiments on existing cards and blank substrates, with costly failures. A first minor successful original card is possible, not fixed. Arc IV Blackstone supplies formal Cardsmith education in mathematics, topology, differential geometry, material science, sigils and practical engineering. Her first **major original Black Card**, Armor of the Abyss, is engineered from blank maege glass in approximately the first third to half of Arc V. This supersedes the older WORKING academic-period Armor placement.

## Resource anchors and material identities

Genesis crystals, Genesis dust and **vaen** can be positioned at sigil anchors to stabilize, reinforce or restructure local manifold topology and energy-channel relationships. Vaen are unusual Genesis-relevant, identity-bearing natural resources, not generic elements. Suitable identity can support a six-to-four-basin optimization, but does not provide an instant upgrade. Exact recipes and technical choices remain OPEN and should drive future crafting scenes.

Vaen can include flora, fungi and other specialty materials from realms, dungeons, domai and localized collapse events. Genesis-infused ordinary metal is distinct from novel higher-order alchemical materials. No catalogue material list or universal ingredient recipe is introduced.

## Living collection and the black mushroom

Kaira recovers an unusual living broad-capped charcoal/black mushroom with glowing Genesis channels among altered flora after the Arc I collapse. It is **vaen, not a Black crystal**. She keeps it as a houseplant, perhaps alongside another living specimen, and may sell other gathered resources. Keeping living vaen does not establish that she can reproduce them.

After restoration she has some sensitivity to Genesis circulation; the perceptual mechanism remains OPEN. The mushroom is **consumed during Armor of the Abyss construction in Arc V**, supplying a useful stabilizing expression. Species, taxonomy, exact effect, complete recipe and preservation details remain OPEN. Not every collected specimen must be consumed.

Later realm expeditions partly serve professional vaen research and collection. The [fungi visual reference](../references/m04-visuals/README.md#visual-register) illustrates possible appearances only; its labels do not identify this mushroom or canonize its biology.

## Milestone 05 workbench professional boundary

**SUPERSEDED:** wording implying a Cardsmith automatically engineers the sigil as well as the card. Architects/Runesmiths design and inscribe the bench sigil; Cardsmiths design/engineer cards and operate professional equipment on the exposed manifold. Shared mathematics and sigil literacy remain. The distinction does not remove access/projection, coupled shadow topology, observation/manipulation, original construction or failure. GF-0011/GF-0019 remain observational and their recorded legacy descriptive mismatch remains unresolved. M04 mushroom consumption and original Armor in Arc V remain intact.
