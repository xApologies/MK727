# Book One Arc One — MK117 chapters 1–5 adaptation

**Status:** WORKING scene sequence. Preserve source narrative architecture; do not mechanically reproduce incompatible details.

## Opening / Chapter 1
Kira age 23, Afflicted, seven years in armorsmith service. Her mother's three Genesis/Card engineering books remain. She manually weaves chainmail for Crown guard equipment. Her one ordinary Fireball Card is treasured; original MK117 lost-card/apothecary recovery scene is a candidate. Apothecary and abeca social ties and the rare relief of delivery journeys remain candidates. She is positive and curious despite poor conditions. Avoid importing MK117 Enix's original mother/education history.

## Delivery / Chapter 2
Blacksmith owns **one valuable donkey and cart** for heavy armor shipments; Kira travels without the original junior blacksmith. Cart material/route geometry inherited from MK147 and uploaded map, not reinvented. Guards respect her fine mail; guard watch/raeon, rare meat, firefly formations, regional distances and local dangers are compatible MK117 material. Mine Hollowmere is only a guard-delivery destination and economic cause of settlement, not a Kira mine exploration plot.

## Collapse / Chapter 2–3
Kira's unusual Afflicted Genesis configuration contributes the final trigger to an already unstable localized collapse; Eldris appear. The exact monster choreography, cart/donkey fate, injuries and route timing require prose adaptation. Kira may fire approximately 2–3 Fireballs before exhaustion and then must survive physically. **No Black Genesis crystal forms or is harvested.** She recovers useful Red–Violet crystals and a very rare naturally formed White crystal (plus possible dust). A White crystal is associated with rare Domai/Genesis-collapse conditions; whether this specific localized event should be classified a Domai must be resolved against AERA rather than assumed.

## Guard outpost alchemist / Chapter 3
A recently arrived woman at a regional guard outpost operates as an alchemist and informal healer, gathering materials and selling potions in a Crown-incentivized remote community. Her ordinary MK117-style healing potion accelerates Kira's own repair, closing wounds but leaving facial and back scarring. It does not cure Affliction. The woman discovers White crystal wealth and recognizes that theft/murder and rumor could doom them both. A nearby specialist healer's absence is consistent with remote development, not evidence the Crown lacks advanced infrastructure.

## Restoration / Chapter 3–4
Standard restoration needs six appropriately cut R–V Genesis crystals, Genesis dust in ru’ne channels, a prepared liquid in a vial and an activated sigil. All six crystals are **consumed** to form a stable White-superposed potion, structurally distinct from a naturally occurring White Genesis crystal. In this exceptional instance the alchemist also **consumes the rare White crystal** as a stabilizing catalyst to remove a dangerous object from circulation. This is not a normal ingredient and is not proof White crystals can be manufactured. Kira's unique biology responds with unusual White/Black Genesis-integrated phenotype and unified Neon access; prior healed scars remain. Do not import MK117's Black crystal stabilizer or MK157's Valnak/Elara transformation.

## Aftermath / Chapter 5
Leftover chromatic cut shards provide modest but meaningful wealth. Kira can leave the exploitative smithy and the village-hub stigma, retain her books and ordinary Card, and move to a city. Chapter 5 of MK117's short master workup was partial: detailed scenes remain OPEN. The intended endpoint is health, independence and possibility, not immediate mastery or riches.
