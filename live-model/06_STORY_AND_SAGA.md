# Seven-book saga — MK117 adaptation plan

**Status:** WORKING story scaffold, not finalized chapters. MK117 is the source for early narrative scenes and social progression, not the governing MK727 magic system. Two author-supplied MK117 PDFs are archived under `references/MK117/`.

## Book One / Red — inherited arc trajectory
- **Village hub:** Kira's exploitative smithy life; treasured Fireball Card and mother's three books; chainmail, apothecary, abeca culture; solo donkey/cart delivery route, guard respect, `raeon` community, Genesis-adapted fireflies, rare meat, local collapse and Eldris, emergency alchemical healing, extraordinary White-catalyzed Affliction restoration, departure and independence.
- **City / Orange District:** first independent lodging, bank, routines, food, abeca house, card shops, artifacts, local friends and social misunderstandings. The original MK117 'first Fireball Card purchase' scene is superseded because Kira already begins with one; preserve useful emotional beats without duplicating acquisition.
- **Chainmail robe movement:** purchase prepared rings; manual multi-link-depth robe design; sigil-applied near-weightlessness; dark iron/gunmetal chainmail robes become a transductionist cultural fashion distinct from colored status robes worn by self-conscious maegi. Commission growth, imitation products, business, social visibility, possible noble relationships. No personal 'learn a concept to cast' progression.
- **Blackstone / academy:** an established higher-education institution, now teaching Genesis mathematics, topology, sigils, material science, and professional Cardsmithing; it does NOT require ordinary card users to learn the entire Transduction lifecycle. Kira's first Black Card, Armor of the Abyss, is crafted/discovered during her academic period (WORKING).
- **Aithren entry:** after academic development Kira **joins as an adventurer**, seeking exploration, combat experience and resources. Her pre-existing Cardsmithing and chainmail identities continue concurrently; she may take local Cardsmith commissions because she is skilled, not because she joins as support staff.

## Later books (chromatic themes, NOT power-up ranks)
1. **Red:** first Black Card, Armor of the Abyss, with limited competence.
2. **Orange:** Continuous Spatial Resolution (CSR), mobility/flight, learning dungeon operations.
3. **Yellow:** Genesis Orbs, initially cannonball-like offensive objects.
4. **Green:** developing Armor/Orb integration; no sixth foundational acquisition required.
5. **Blue:** Halo, developing energy throughput/sustainment.
6. **Violet:** Domain, extended Genesis-field interaction.
7. **White:** integrated competence and culmination, not a new mandatory card.

These are planned acquisition locations, not guaranteed book endings. Mastery can lag by several books; Armor mastery can mature near Book Six, Orbs can evolve into direct Transduction interfaces near Books Four–Five, and the integrated battle system culminates in Seven. The sixth Black slot is OPEN. No artificial sixth system should be introduced solely to fill the slot.

## Adaptation firewall
MK117's old Enix origin, junior blacksmith companion, Black crystal and Black-stabilized potion, personal concept learning, and automatic healer/hospital pipeline do not transfer. MK117's hospital arc may be replaced with professional Cardsmithing, while its community/noble/friendship scenes remain candidates. The expeditionary dungeon world is inherited, but Kira's motivation is **becoming an adventurer**, not compulsory professional placement.
