# Corridors, gates and professional duties — Milestone 05

Source: [M05 corridor decisions](../provenance/milestone-05/decisions/03_CORRIDORS.md). **AUTHOR-APPROVED narrative-level design.** This chooses equipment and civic behavior without deriving new L0/L1 mathematics, assigning catalogue IDs or fixing range, price or physics. Source boundaries are recorded in the [M05 source register](../provenance/MILESTONE_05_SOURCE_REGISTER.md).

## Professional separation

| Professional role | Product or duty |
| --- | --- |
| Architects/Runesmiths | Design and inscribe sigils, gates and anchors |
| Cardsmiths | Design and engineer Genesis Cards |
| Transductionists | Operate equipment |

Shared mathematics does not make these products or duties interchangeable. Earlier descriptions implying Cardsmiths automatically design both sigils and cards, or treating the two professional labels as interchangeable, are **SUPERSEDED** for MK727. This does not alter the Transduction lifecycle or erase Kaira's mathematical/sigil literacy. No additional professional qualification is inferred from her being a Cardsmith.

Kaira's [workbench](18_CARDSMITHING_WORKSPACE.md) uses an **architect-made sigil** and **Cardsmith-operated professional cards**. The sigil supplies coupled projection/access; the cards and operator perform observation/manipulation. Cardsmithing research, failure, original blank-glass construction and the Arc V Armor event remain as established in M04. Upstream AERA leaves exact routine civic trade organization open; the role division here is a local author choice, not an upstream amendment.

## Permanent capital gates

The four [royal-domain capitals](22_CIVILIZATION_AND_WILDS.md#four-royal-domains) have permanent transit gates: monumental freestanding stone portals through which another capital-city environment is clearly visible. Walking through is a fluid, doorway-like transition. Approximately **300 m high and 100 m wide** is **ILLUSTRATIVE**. The gates are maintained civic infrastructure; exact routing between gates, throughput, security and mathematical construction remain unspecified.

## One-anchor leapfrog travel

The mobile method uses **ONE reusable mobile sigil anchor and ONE Corridor Card**. There is no second portable anchor.

1. A scout carries the anchor ahead, possibly using CSR flight. The card operator stays with the traveling group.
2. The scout places and activates the anchor.
3. The cardholder discovers the active anchor's projected field interface, completes a compatibility/topological handshake and opens the corridor.
4. Guards may pass first to secure arrival. The group, wagons, animals and cargo follow, subject to capacity.
5. The card is deactivated and the corridor closes. The group rests while the **same anchor** is moved ahead again, then the process repeats.

An inscribed metal cube small enough for a dimensional ring is **possible**, not mandatory. This does not grant Kaira a ring before her tournament award, CSR mastery in Red or a fixed personal travel loadout. Range depends on anchor, card, operator and conditions; exact range, capacity, energy/fatigue demands, card ID/basins and price remain OPEN. The protocol specifies neither a second portable destination device nor instantaneous travel to an unplaced anchor.

## Civilian and military compatibility

Standard civilian anchors/cards are generally interoperable. Other compatible users, including bandits, may interface with an active civilian anchor. **This does not grant automatic control of an existing corridor.** Discovery and a compatible handshake are distinct from taking over another operator's connection.

Military Architects make restricted anchor sigil topology; military Cardsmiths make matching specialist Corridor Cards. Together these provide an access-controlled handshake. Exact security/encryption, discovery, simultaneous contention and interruption behavior remain OPEN. The field-topology analogy does not establish real cryptographic strength or an unbreakable military system.

## Roads and security remain necessary

Roads, wagons, ships, patrols and waystations remain important. Corridors move travelers; they do not patrol skipped territory. Caravan guards secure anchor sites. Bandits might discover a compatible anchor but risk confronting hardened adventurers or Crown guards. No new bandit encounter, trafficking route or shortcut across the unavailable political map is authored here.

Capital gates and field corridors do not settle the separate M04 questions about chromatic-realm gateways, interworld coordinates or native-realm compatibility. Physical anchors, sigils and Architect-created velis retain their distinct source meanings.
