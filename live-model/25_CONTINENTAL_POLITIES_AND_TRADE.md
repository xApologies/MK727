## Three continental polities

The northern continent above the **Eastern Whirlpool** has three broad polities, west to east:

| Position | Descriptive polity label | Established social and material direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| West | Kaira's Crown Kingdom | Monarchy, free-market wage economy, nobles and the existing four royal domains |
| Center | Seven Merchant Houses polity | Seven ruling merchant families; concentrated ownership/wealth and indentured or contract-dependent labor; seven monumental multilevel metropolises of spatially locked stone |
| East | Warrior kingdom | Eldris-fighting martial culture, belief in eternal paradise, semi-independent military fiefdoms and huge phase-grown metal pyramids |

These labels do not assign new proper kingdom or family names. The supplied yellow boundary strokes are **APPROXIMATE**, not surveyed borders. The Crown's four royal domains are subdivisions of the western kingdom, not the three continental polities or seven merchant cities. Earlier M04 wording treating three kingdoms on a continent as merely ILLUSTRATIVE is **SUPERSEDED for this identified continent only**; its illustrative cartel counts stay illustrative.

The eastern pyramids' exact purpose and military command hierarchy remain OPEN. The merchant city's size was described both as **hundreds of square miles** and **hundreds of miles in radius**. Those are not equivalent measures: neither is selected or converted into a fixed area. Exact metropolitan scale remains OPEN and does not replace the Crown's M05 WORKING city footprints/radii.

## abecca and commodity exchange

**Canonical lowercase spelling: `abecca` (A-B-E-C-C-A).** Author voice “coffee” refers to it. Older `abeca` is a historical spelling, not a second plant. Current prose-facing references use `abecca`.

Botanical concept: a roughly **three-inch pine-nut-like husk**, dense inner fiber and **four seeds in the center**. The seeds are dried and roasted for the drink; the fiber is milked/pulped into a thick, slightly sweet and bitter plant cream. Taxonomy, yields and exact preparation quantities remain OPEN. Eldris pressure limits large livestock and dairy without absolutely forbidding them.

abecca originated outside the Crown Kingdom and is now an ordinary staple there. It grows in multiple world regions with distinctive flavor profiles; exact origin places, varieties and species names remain OPEN. Broad commodity exchange includes fruit, plants, seeds, cloth, dyes, pigments, specialty lumber, vaen, crystals and manufactured goods. Availability, cost, consumer desire and manufacturing specialization shape import/export. Animal importation remains OPEN.

Kingdoms may restrict or ban the import/export of protected species/materials and drugs; illicit demand can sustain black markets. This establishes possible policy, not a particular ban, tariff, smuggling route or recipe.

## Timber extraction and protected forestry

Extensive forests coexist with **globally costly timber** because safe extraction and transport through Genesis-risk Wilds are scarce. Eldris and collapse pressure create costs; forest abundance does not guarantee accessible lumber. Two complementary models are established directions:

1. Temporary Crown-led armed extraction: guards, Architects and local stabilizing anchors support a shipbuilding harvest, then withdraw. A requirement for **five ships is ILLUSTRATIVE**, not an order, fixed yield or standing fleet.
2. Expensive permanently protected timber villages/cities: Architect-maintained **velis** accelerate tree growth, alchemically enriched soil supports nutrient demand, and harvesting, milling, security and export income sustain the specialization.

Growth is neither free nor infinite. A velis is an Architect-created Genesis-field topology, distinct from a physical sigil or mobile corridor anchor; maintenance and material/nutrient demand still matter. The temporary extraction model's exact anchor equipment is OPEN, not automatically M05's Corridor Card setup. Regional premium woods include naturally black timber, walnut-like species and other distinct woods; names and exact origins remain OPEN.

## Stone, joinery and employment

Ordinary stone/mineral blocks, including marble and sandstone, are phase-grown through sigil arrays in precise orthogonal forms and spatially locked. Large-scale construction is inexpensive **relative to timber**, not free. Exact material economics, rates, prices and energy costs remain OPEN. This infrastructure preserves the M05 separation: Architects/Runesmiths design sigils; Cardsmiths engineer cards.

Elite all-wood mansions and fine dovetail/structural joinery are extreme status symbols; master carpenters are highly paid. Ships and shipyards consume expensive timber. Alternative metal or stone ships are **not yet established**.

The supply chain supports forestry, alchemy, Architects, soldiers, guards, haulers, roads, waystations, wagons, mills, carpenters, shipwrights, dock workers, sails, ropes and maintenance. This is an employment/economic model, not an invented route or shipping manifest.

## International shipping and cost

Intercontinental cargo fleets are very expensive: timber, crew, maintenance, guards, storms, wrecks, piracy, provisions and long routes all contribute. A merchant house owning **three cargo ships** and hiring **five escort ships** is **ILLUSTRATIVE**, not standard composition. Merchant-owned fleets, professional guard fleets, Crown navies and pirate fleets are distinct.

A **seven-month round trip** or a **two-year voyage** is ILLUSTRATIVE. Exact routes, duration and ship sizes remain OPEN. Existing Transduction Cards might manipulate wind/water within their established scope; no new propulsion system, card ID or basin assignment is created. Ships sustain shipwright, security, transport and port employment. Consumers may bear some freight/security/risk costs in prices; profitable trade is not guaranteed.

## Piracy and illicit markets

Pirates maintain major high-seas floating settlements and fleets, continuing the broad Genesis Point inheritance already accepted in M04. Detailed architecture and hierarchy remain OPEN; old sanctuary geometry and volcanic Pirate-Island geography are not imported.

Pirates may demand coercive tolls in **both directions** and permit passage to preserve recurring income. They may instead board, take rare goods, seize ships, fight or break promises. **A toll never guarantees safe passage.** Individual captains may be unpredictable and theatrical rather than uniformly rational; escorts therefore retain value.

Pirates can supply illegally exported/imported protected goods; cartels and black-market brokers connect buyers and sellers. Existing slave and drug trafficking remains background. No new rates, exact illicit routes, recipes, hierarchy or operational procedures are specified. The [M04 foreground-emphasis matrix](19_ANTAGONISTS.md#book-emphasis-matrix) is unchanged, as are independent faction objectives and Kaira's non-use of the unnamed fictional drug.
