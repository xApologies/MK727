## Material identity and scope

Elemental Transduction addresses specified material identities, chemical compositions and permitted configuration states. It does not mean classical fire/earth/air/water elements. Manipulating water H2O does not automatically permit independent hydrogen nuclear operations; manipulating an atmospheric mixture does not grant unrestricted control over each gas's nucleus. Material target, permitted transformations and operator competency are separate constraints.

## Proposed industrial family and economics

| Required basin count | Proposed design | Availability and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two | Element-specific manipulation, such as Fe, Cu, Ti, H or O | Many standardized varieties; relatively affordable professional equipment, still expensive to ordinary people. Specific scope alone does not establish power. |
| Three | General material-family manipulation, including General Metal Manipulation | Several standardized variants, significantly more expensive. Iron/steel compatibility is proposed; the exact compatible metals/alloys, including copper and titanium, remain OPEN. |
| Six | Advanced universal-general material-family card | Mass-produced, broadly compatible, very expensive: a luxury or high-end capital asset. “Universal-general” describes the proposed material-family envelope, not unrestricted nuclear or all-matter control. |

A skilled Cardsmith may engineer an advanced six-basin card to require four while preserving its intended manipulation envelope, using crystals, dust, vaen and substantial expertise at very high cost. A thermal card might normally require three basins and be optimized to two. These are **ILLUSTRATIVE engineering targets**, not guaranteed compression laws, automatic upgrades or a compulsory progression ladder. Exact IDs, basin colors, compatible material lists, recipes and Eln prices are OPEN.

Price growth is intended to be steep/superlinear or approximately exponential within this proposed family. No multiplier or numeric price is approved. This does not replace the published catalogue's pricing classes or make basin count a measure of raw power, fatigue or professional rank. Craftsmanship, Transductionist competency and equipment architecture are independent professional axes.

## Heating, sequencing and concurrency

A six-basin general metal card can shape stock heated by a physical forge. A smith may instead activate a thermal card, heat stock, deactivate it to release its basins, activate the metal card and shape while the heat persists, repeating as needed. Releasing thermal Shaping does not erase heat already deposited.

Concurrent ordinary cards require **disjoint exact basin sets** under [existing card operation](01_MAGIC.md). A four-basin metal card plus a two-basin thermal card can coexist only when their assigned colors do not overlap; counts summing to six are insufficient. No new colors are assigned here. Mixed Black/ordinary concurrency remains OPEN.

## Kaira's prepared-ring learning path

The author-selected **proposed first purchase** is a three-basin General Metal Manipulation card. Kaira buys professionally manufactured prepared rings from a local smith on Blacksmith Row, preserving the supplier relationship. A batch of 20,000 rings is ILLUSTRATIVE, not an approved order quantity.

In Arc II she learns small controlled operations: opening, orienting, threading/interlocking and closing rings, eventually assembling multiple rings. Seven years of manual chainmail skill supplies pattern knowledge, fit and judgment; she is initially a novice in Transduction control and fumbles before improving. Manual and assisted steps can coexist. Later Thermal Manipulation and complete wire/ingot processing are future developments, not prerequisites for her first robe.

Her mother's ordinary Fireball remains sentimental defense; no late first-Fireball purchase or Enix biography transfers from MK117. Expanded Genesis Sight is an attractive observational option, not a forced acquisition. The [workspace specification](18_CARDSMITHING_WORKSPACE.md) distinguishes observation from editing; [Book Red expansion](21_M04_BOOK_RED_EXPANSION.md) preserves the learning chronology.
