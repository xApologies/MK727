# Kira's Black Genesis Cards — saga foundation

**Status:** Five systems and unified access ESTABLISHED; book placements and exact manufacturing events WORKING. Source inspiration MK157; no Valnak, Black Binding purchase gates or old credit ladder.

## Access
AERA/MK157's White/Black Möbius analogy describes two presentations of a continuous Neon structure: White is superposition of actual R–V states, Black is unified Neon (bounded 0–1), not six separate Black channels. Kira's restored Genesis-integrated biology permits access. Ordinary people may physically possess a Black Card but cannot pull from unified Neon and therefore cannot fully operate it. Her Cardsmith expertise allows her to design Black-compatible manifolds; a reproducible method remains to be developed in prose. Black Cards are not automatically salable as universal magic items, so they do not give her unlimited income.

Kira can own many Black Cards but maintain **no more than six active simultaneously** (MK727 book-specific concurrency). Do not impose ordinary chromatic basin-conflict logic on these cards. Whether Black and ordinary card activations share a combined total remains OPEN; do not silently grant >6 simultaneous total activations. Access does not equal mastery, infinite realized output, unlimited cognition, or zero fatigue.

## Five foundational cards
1. **Armor of the Abyss** — body-linked regenerative protective architecture, evolving claws and reshaping; armor repair is not biological healing. Early use is primarily armor/claws; sustained draw fatigues her. Learned reconfiguration and full combat mastery develop across books.
2. **CSR — Continuous Spatial Resolution** — spatial support, positioning, eventual flight; not automatic mass offense. Use `Continuous`, not `Controlled`, for this specific inherited Black system.
3. **Genesis Orbs** — initially cannonball-like offensive bodies; eventual consciously controlled external Transduction interfaces. Direct conceptual Transduction through Orbs is an advanced **learned** skill, not how ordinary cards work. Range/velocity/count/control are constrained by cognition, knowledge, sustainment and context; do not assign a fixed maximum without author decision.
4. **Halo** — three WORKING throughput modes: compact ~1.7×, expanded ~3×, large ~5× **energy draw/handling**, not energy creation. Greater throughput can support Black-system intensity and reduce some sustainment burden; exact fatigue law OPEN. Ordinary capped Shapings do not become stronger merely from wastefully supplying excess energy.
5. **Domain** — extends Kira's Genesis-field interaction outward, enabling later composition with Orbs, Armor, CSR and Halo; not automatic total environmental control.

## Integration
Book Seven's endpoint is coordinated use of the five systems, not omnipotence. Kira still has control, cognition, fatigue, throughput and tactical limits. Ordinary chromatic cards remain accessible and valuable in the broader world; how they coexist concurrently with Black Cards needs an explicit rule before combat scenes mix them.
