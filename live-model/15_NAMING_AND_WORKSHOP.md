# Naming pool and AERA workshop application
**AERA source:** `L2/LANGUAGE/AE_AI_NAMING_CONVENTION.md` — masculine AE and feminine AI are **general optional tendencies**, not gender rules. Existing names and vocabulary take priority. Strong consonants K/L/M/N/R/T/V preferred, B/C selectively; generally avoid J/Y initials. Reader-friendly first-glance readability is the principal editorial filter. One syllable predominant, two acceptable, three exceptional. Similar-sounding names and repeated endings are **allowed**; do not purge on similarity alone.

**Canon spelling:** protagonist is **Kaira** (K-A-I-R-A) in manuscript; **Kira** is author-facing shorthand to accommodate voice-to-text. Both refer to the same person. Kaira may also appear in the candidate name pool; do not delete merely because protagonist already uses it. Do not canonize any other name solely by its presence in the pool.

**Candidate pool:** `catalogues/names/names.csv` and `names.txt` contain the 185-name post-audit library (93 feminine, 92 masculine). Prior author-approved cleanup removed 15 problematic names, including `Vaen` (collides with `vaen` resource vocabulary). Remaining awkward candidates can simply go unused. Pen may propose/assign names while writing; Ink approves characters and canon; Page tracks consistency.
