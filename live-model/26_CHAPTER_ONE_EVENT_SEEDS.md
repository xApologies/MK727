## Salve errand and collision

Kaira, age **23 and Afflicted**, works for the exploitative master armorsmith. Prolonged manual chainmail-ring labor leaves her fingertips raw. The master sends her to an apothecary for **ordinary topical salve so she can keep producing**, not from compassion. This salve is not an Affliction cure, the later emergency healing potion or the exceptional restoration.

The apothecary woman is married to a **subordinate armorsmith working for the master**. Her husband is not a newly assigned delivery apprentice or companion. On the return toward the smithy, Kaira notices an irritable baker shouting at people near his fresh pastry display. She crosses the street to avoid his Affliction-based hostility. Distracted, she knocks down a visiting noble's son, who is furious that an Afflicted commoner touched him.

The collision seeds a recurring **seven-book rivalry**. Kaira often outdoes him, and later knows the princess, who outranks him. This does not assign a royal-introduction date, noble rank/domain, dialogue, revenge scene or fixed encounter in every book. The rivalry complements the established faction arcs; it is not a new all-controlling antagonist organization.
