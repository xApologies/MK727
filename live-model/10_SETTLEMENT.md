Remote settlement does not mean uniformly shabby or lawless.

Its proximity is the reason the Crown is developing the region. It sits away from the central hub; Kira's story uses it only as an occasional armor-delivery destination for stationed guards. Mining detail, production rates, monster pressure specifics and map-derived exact distances are inherited or OPEN, not reasons to invent a mining arc.
