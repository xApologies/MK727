# Blackstone, Cardsmithing, chainmail couture and Aithren

## Blackstone
Blackstone's MK147 identity and institution are inherited. MK727 replaces the idea that each practitioner must learn a spell's concept to cast: **the card executes the entire six-stage Transduction lifecycle**. Higher education develops domain expertise. Healers study Genesis Field fundamentals, chemistry, biology, anatomy, diagnosis, pathology, clinical triage and potentially residency. Architects/Cardsmiths study geometry, topology, Genesis mathematics, sigils, material science, crystals, vaen, manifolds, design and fabrication. Ordinary combatants can become expert through practical drills and expeditions without university education. A Fireball Card in novice hands and master hands is the same Shaping but radically different targeting, cadence, control and endurance.

## Kira's professional identities accumulate
Kira is simultaneously (1) daughter/apprentice of a Cardsmith, (2) extraordinary manual chainmail craftswoman, (3) designer/owner of a chainmail robe business, (4) developing educated Cardsmith/Architect, (5) private Black Card researcher, and eventually (6) an `aithren` adventurer. Becoming an adventurer does **not** erase her professions or convert her entry motive into staffing a dungeon town. She enters to explore and develop as a delver; may use Cardsmithing for friends/clients because it is already part of her identity.

## Chainmail robe movement
Kira first buys prepared rings, weaves robes by hand, and uses established sigil-based material modification to make thick, dark iron/gunmetal multi-link-depth robes feel feather-light. This is material engineering, **not** a new card Shaping concept. Robes become a cultural identity for transductionists/card users without advertising Red–White social assessment rank, in contrast with rank-colored maegi robes. Kira unintentionally provokes some status-conscious mages. Fashion commissions, imitation products and a workshop supply independent income that subsidizes card research and expedition life. Do not assume fashion popularity or production scale before scenes establish them.

Early: metal stock → mechanically drawn wire → mandrel winding → ring cutting → hand closures → hand weaving → robe. Later: Kira crafts/obtains heat and metal manipulation cards, learns coordinated thermal conditioning, wire shaping, winding, separating, interlocking and closing; can combine manual and card-assisted steps. She supplies chainmail pattern knowledge, geometry, fit, articulation and judgment; cards execute prepared physical Shapings. Exact catalogue entries/basins for these examples must be looked up before prose asserts them. She can eventually run an entire Genesis-assisted chainmail production line, becoming a conductor of matter without losing love for manual craft.
