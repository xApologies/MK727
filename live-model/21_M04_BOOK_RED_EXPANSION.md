# Book Red expansion — Milestone 04

**Source:** [M04 Book Red decisions](../provenance/milestone-04/decisions/01_BOOK_RED.md), with [material](17_MATERIAL_MANIPULATION.md), [workspace](18_CARDSMITHING_WORKSPACE.md), [antagonist](19_ANTAGONISTS.md) and [realm](20_REALMS_AND_STARSCAPE.md) specifications. Author-approved beats, proposed equipment and OPEN choreography remain distinct. The [six-arc spine](12_BOOK_RED_SIX_ARCS.md) remains the sequence authority.

## Pacing and cumulative identities

The author's long-form high-fantasy aim is approximately **900–1300 pages per book**, with substantial slice-of-life, relationships, character development and worldbuilding. This is a pacing aspiration, not a fixed manuscript length or chapter allocation. It is not a compressed power-up ladder. Book colors are narrative labels, not realm or basin assignments.

Kaira remains a chainmail craftswoman, designer/business owner, Cardsmith, Black Card researcher and independent adventurer. Learning and acquisition do not guarantee mastery. Scenes, dates, routes, supporting names and exact chapter counts remain OPEN.

## Arc I — Village Hub

Preserve age 23; Cardsmith mother died at 15; seven years at the smithy from 16–23; sleeping by the forge, one evening meal, minimal wage and punitive deductions. Her three maternal technical books and **mother-made ordinary Fireball Card** survive as treasured possessions. About two or three shots exhaust her in the collapse confrontation; ID and basin requirements remain OPEN.

Manual chainmail and secret roadside harvesting occur during deliveries with the **smith-owned donkey/cart**, without a junior apprentice. During the localized collapse she recovers R–V and rare White crystals, altered flora and a living black mushroom vaen. The exceptional restoration consumes the six chromatic crystals and White catalyst; there is **no Black crystal**. Emergency healing repairs injuries but leaves scars, which restoration does not erase.

She may sell some specimens and keeps the mushroom, perhaps with another living specimen, as houseplants. Its broad charcoal/black cap and glowing Genesis channels are the visual direction; taxonomy, species, extra specimen and event choreography remain OPEN. It is later consumed in Arc V Armor engineering, not in the restoration potion. Exact collapse classification, donkey/cart fate and scene routes remain unresolved.

## Arc II — Orange District

Independent city life develops through abeca, Raeon, shopkeepers and Blacksmith Row. Kaira buys prepared chainmail rings from a local smith; 20,000 is an illustrative quantity. The proposed three-basin **General Metal Manipulation** first purchase supports opening, orienting, interlocking and closing rings. She brings expert manual craft but novice Transduction control: fumbles and practice matter.

The earlier hand-woven robe description remains the manual foundation. Card-assisted ring assembly begins here and can mix with handwork; later thermal/wire/ingot mastery remains future development. This explicitly supersedes reading the older “later cards” wording as postponing all assisted assembly until after Blackstone. Her Fireball remains sentimental defense. Expanded Genesis Sight is attractive early observation, not a mandatory acquisition.

## Arc III — Chainmail Robe Movement

Commissions, independent income, social identity and a growing professional toolkit develop alongside robe fashion. The king owning a robe is author-supplied background; precise commission and royal-introduction scenes remain OPEN.

Early maternal teaching is not a completed qualification. Kaira experiments with the sigil workbench, modifying existing cards and engineering blank maege glass, sometimes fracturing costly cards. A first minor original success is possible, not fixed. She collects living vaen as plants and senses some Genesis circulation after restoration; perceptual mechanism and reproduction remain OPEN. Finesse and energy-field configuration control partly transfer from rings to Black Armor and later Orbs without guaranteeing mastery.

## Arc IV — Blackstone education

Formal Cardsmith education develops mathematics, topology, differential geometry, material science, sigils and practical engineering. **Armor construction is not placed here.** The earlier WORKING Blackstone/academic-period manufacturing event is explicitly SUPERSEDED by Arc V. Ordinary card operation still does not require constructing a card's underlying concept.

## Arc V — Aithren and original Armor

Kaira voluntarily joins **to adventure**: dungeon-delving, friendships, small Cardsmith commissions and team formation. Approximately the **first third to half of Arc V** contains the original construction of **Armor of the Abyss**, her first major original Black Card, from blank maege glass. The black mushroom is consumed as a special stabilizing vaen. The exact recipe, embedding mechanism and stabilizing effect are OPEN.

The rest of the arc develops armor use, delving, combat competency, relationships and the tournament team. This flexible pacing does not assign a chapter, fixed duration or exact order to every friendship/commission scene. Academic competence and crafting success do not automatically supply combat mastery.

## Arc VI — Team tournament and divergence

The King's Tournament is a team competition. Kaira's team finishes **AUTHOR-LOCKED Yellow/fourth place**, not outright victory. Kaira receives a **high-end Dimensional Ring and monetary prize after competition**, at the end of Red. No ring is available in earlier arcs or during the tournament. Yellow placement does not set the ring's quality color, change source dungeon scoring or assign a basin rank.

The ring is finite recursive-topology conservation with conscious identity selection, not a pocket dimension, unlimited storage or an inventory UI. Exact tier, capacity and cash sum remain OPEN. Her future core traveling kit can include cards, mother's books, tools, chainmail stock, bedroll and provisions. A compact portable Cardsmith table is only a future possibility, not awarded equipment or an established capability.

After the team's achievement, companions accept professional **vaelum** employment. Kaira declines to pursue independent Cardsmithing and exploration. Friendship continues and later reunion is possible. Team names, employer identity and exact scenes remain OPEN; professional divergence is not a friendship rupture.

## Orange through White

The ring supports travel and realm-vaen expeditions. The inherited **WORKING** scaffold remains Armor in Red, CSR in Orange, Orbs in Yellow, Green development, Halo in Blue, Domain in Violet and integration in White. No sixth Black Card is mandated. Red's Arc V Armor event is now author-established; the later acquisition/mastery scaffold remains provisional.

Late Orange/early Yellow and later realm exploration can take substantial book space without matching realm colors to book labels. [Four human antagonist pressures](19_ANTAGONISTS.md#book-emphasis-matrix) escalate independently from the environmental crisis. White's author-established planetary stabilization ends collapse and collapse-driven monster emergence; existing Eldris fate remains OPEN.

## Milestone 05 cumulative continuity

Hollowmere #6 is a **Green-rated persistent dungeon**, superseding all Yellow-mine references. The planned hub remains in place near mountains/runoff and lives around the dungeon economy. Arc I's existing five-chapter working sequence is preserved: Kaira delivers armor to dungeon guards/adventuring support without entering Hollowmere. She has admired its community for years. Arc V adventuring elsewhere, team and Armor fulfill that interest; Arc VI returns her with the team for her **first entry into Hollowmere**. Exact race stage/date/reunions remain OPEN. Six arcs, Armor first third-to-half V, Yellow/fourth and high-end ring/cash after competition remain unchanged.

Her workbench sigil is architect-made; Cardsmithing concerns card engineering with shared mathematics and professional operation. An accepted world atlas exists as an author decision but its bytes and political overlay are missing. Arc II's exact city remains OPEN. Capital gates and the generic optional ring-sized anchor do not grant Kaira early ring/CSR use.

Red is the first continuous volume of seven with a satisfying identity-formation arc, not deliberately standalone or required to close all sequel threads. [Background illicit economies](19_ANTAGONISTS.md#milestone-05-background-underworld) can be acknowledged in ordinary conversation while later books foreground the M04 factions. Kaira does not use the unnamed fictional drug; no scenes, names or routes are added.
