## Resolved integration tensions

- **Arc IV versus V Armor:** retained historical text is visibly marked with current M04 authority. Current spine and cumulative notes place formal education in IV, original Armor fabrication in approximately the first third to half of V, with mushroom consumption and subsequent practice. No accidental acquisition/mastery equivalence.
- **Tournament status:** all current summaries lock team Yellow/fourth and high-end ring/cash after competition. Earlier OPEN-result statements remain explicitly superseded history. Exact ring color/tier, capacity and cash remain OPEN; inherited point values are not finish-place rewards.
- **Manual versus card-assisted robes:** 08 and the expansion describe manual experience followed by novice assisted prepared-ring assembly, with a continuing supplier. Thermal/full stock processing remains later. Neither manual identity nor new card direction is silently deleted.
- **80/20 optionality:** AERA requires structural energy basins, while MK727 locally chooses inhabited realms and the approximate proportions. White's ~one-sixth illustration is not promoted to universal equilibrium mathematics.
- **Planet scale and environmental ending:** old ~60,000-mile circumference stays historical; MK727's ~80,000-mile diameter is author scale direction with physics OPEN. White stabilization ends collapse-driven emergence, not an invented elimination of all existing life.
- **Scarring:** M03's unsupported stronger phrase “healing potion causes scarring” remains historical. The current handoff preserves the verified earlier meaning: repair leaves existing injuries scarred; restoration retains them.
