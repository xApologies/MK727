## 04. Timber, stone and labor economy
Extensive forests exist but safe timber extraction in the Wilds is dangerous because of Eldris and Genesis-collapse pressure. Timber is globally costly because secure harvesting/transport is scarce. Two complementary models: (a) temporary Crown-led armed extraction with guards, Architects and local stabilizing anchors to harvest shipbuilding stock then withdraw; illustrative requirement five ships; (b) expensive permanently protected specialized timber villages/cities with Architect-maintained `velis` for accelerated tree growth, alchemically enriched soil to support nutrients, harvesting, milling, security and export revenues. Accelerated growth not free/infinite. Regional premium woods include naturally black timber, walnut-like species and other distinct woods.
