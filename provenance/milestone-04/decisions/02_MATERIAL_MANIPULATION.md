# Proposed industrial material-manipulation card family
**Status:** AUTHOR-APPROVED DESIGN DIRECTION, catalogue entries/IDs and exact basin color sets OPEN. Preserve existing 1245 records and assignments unchanged. No universal alteration of MK727 pricing classes.

Elemental Transduction means control over specified material identities, chemical compositions and permitted configuration states, NOT classical fire/earth/air/water elements. Water H2O manipulation does not automatically allow independent hydrogen nuclear operations; atmospheric mixture manipulation does not imply unrestricted control of each gas's nucleus. Material target, permitted transformations and operator competency are independent.

**Two-basin**: element-specific manipulation (Fe, Cu, Ti, H, O, etc.), many standardized varieties; relatively affordable professional cards, still expensive to ordinary people. Specific element control is not inherently more or less powerful than broad control. **Three-basin**: general material-family manipulation (e.g. General Metal Manipulation), several standardized variants, significantly more expensive; supports a useful compatible set of metals and alloys (iron/steel proposed; exact copper/titanium/etc list OPEN). **Six-basin**: mass-produced advanced universal-general material-family card, very expensive (luxury/high-end capital asset), broadly compatible. Skilled Cardsmith may modify advanced six-basin card to **four** while preserving intended manipulation envelope using crystals/dust/vaen and engineering, at very high cost. A thermal manipulation card might normally occupy three basins, and be optimized to two, allowing 4+2 distinct basins concurrently if color assignments do not overlap. These numbers are illustrative engineering targets, not automatic universal compression guarantees. Price growth steep/superlinear or approximately exponential, but no numeric multiplier or Eln amounts approved.

A six-basin general metal card may be used with a **physical forge** for heating. Other smiths can **sequence** thermal card on → heat stock → deactivate/free basins → metal card on → shape while heat persists, repeating as needed. Concurrent operation only if basin requirements disjoint; counts summing to six are not sufficient by themselves. Released thermal Shaping does not erase deposited heat. Do not conflate required basin count with raw power, fatigue or professional rank.

Her manual seven-year chainmail skill does not grant immediate card competency. Later Thermal Manipulation and full wire/ingot processing are future developments. Professional rank has independent axes: craftsmanship, Transductionist competency, equipment architecture.
