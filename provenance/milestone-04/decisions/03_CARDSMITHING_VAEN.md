# Cardsmith workspace, professional toolkit and vaen
**Author direction:** The Cardsmith's orthogonal sigil workbench is an **access/projection workspace**, NOT an automatic editing tool. A physical maege-glass card anchors its embedded Genesis manifold. Activating the sigil produces an isolated phase-state shadow projection of embedded topology in an accessible working field. Turning the sigil off removes projection/access; card remains card. Professional Genesis Cards, operated by an Architect/Cardsmith, supply **observation and manipulation** of the accessible field. Operator supplies math, competency, judgment. Incorrect changes can break topology and spider-fracture/ruin maege glass. Do not describe workbench on/off as a separate editing action. Shadow projection is coupled to underlying card, not an independent copy.

Existing MK727 catalogue candidate **Expanded Genesis Sight GF-0011** for observation, **Expanded Transduction Trace GF-0019** for residual analysis; their existing functions do NOT automatically include editing. Other professional interaction/editing cards are proposed, IDs and complete toolkit OPEN. Cardsmiths/Architects may use similar cards to interact with local topology without workbench; workbench specifically exposes a card's embedded manifold. Differentiate an existing card's manifold modification from **blank maege-glass original construction**; blank substrate has no embedded Shaping, so initialization/embedding mechanism requires specification. Cardsmith's first experiments can fail and be expensive; Kaira learns informally from mother's three books and childhood instruction, then university at Blackstone.

Genesis crystals, Genesis dust and **`vaen`** (unusual Genesis-relevant identity-bearing natural resources, not generic elements) can be positioned at sigil anchor locations to stabilize, reinforce, restructure local manifold topology and energy-channel relationships. Suitable vaen identity may support a six→four-basin card optimization without being a magic instant upgrade. Exact resource recipes OPEN; crafting materials and technical decisions drive scenes. Genesis-infused ordinary metal differs from novel higher-order alchemical materials. Vaen may be flora, fungi, other specialty materials from realms, dungeons, domai and local collapse events.

Kaira collects living vaen as houseplants; not necessarily able to reproduce them. She keeps an unusual broad-capped charcoal/black mushroom with glowing Genesis channels discovered after Arc I collapse, possibly other flora, and senses some Genesis circulation due to transformation. **The black mushroom is consumed later during Armor of the Abyss engineering in Arc V**, and provides a useful stabilizing expression; exact effect/species OPEN. She may sell other gathered materials. Later realm exploration is driven partly by collecting vaen for professional Cardsmithing; not every collected specimen must be consumed.
