# MK727 realm and starscape realization
AERA six R–V energy basins and White superposition are mandatory structural existence, but **inhabited/traversable realms are OPTIONAL AERA implementations**. MK147 may use basins only. MK727 **chooses** six inhabitable chromatic realms, plus White material realm, using an approximate **80% dominant basin + 20% scaffold** from other five, with residual proportions not necessarily equal. White is balanced six-basin superposition (~1/6 each as author illustration). Colors are NOT fire/water/nature elemental categories; book colors are NOT forced matching realm itinerary. No fixed realm visit sequence.

**Geometry:** Six realms can have non-ordinary topology and differential geometry, multiple locally oriented traversable landscapes, 'sky' as another ground/ocean, connecting mountains and local up/down changes. Example image overhead ocean waterfalls is a visual reference, not universal shape. Locally changing effective gravity is not proof of nonorientability; exact physics, metric, hydrology and transport OPEN.

**Compatibility author rule:** White-native humans/denizens can ordinarily exist in all six chromatic realms and White. A native Red denizen can ordinarily inhabit Red and White only; similarly Orange→Orange+White, Yellow→Yellow+White, Green→Green+White, Blue→Blue+White, Violet→Violet+White. Compatibility does NOT grant automatic travel. Realm gateways and route mechanics OPEN. Sprites are already generalized in MK727 `02_WORLD.md`: small playful chromatic energy entities; collective/hive may assume forms, potentially humanlike for White travel. Exact ontology, embodiment, lifespan, mechanisms OPEN. Do not impose unverified species rules.

**Starscape:** Chromatic realms serve as traversable interworld bridge environments; multiple planets, potentially other galaxies, may access the same realms. Human traveler may enter realm from one White world and eventually reach another world, with actual passage/coordinates/duration still OPEN. Kaira's late Orange / early Yellow and later exploration can occupy major portions of books, driven by vaen research, exploration, encounters and profession. Planetary world map not yet provided. Realm choice follows story, not book color.

**Sources:** AERA `CONCEPTS/01_SEVEN_REALMS.md` explicitly says 80/20 is optional example, not universal; `L2/ENVIRONMENT/01_DOMAIN_CONTEXT.md` distinguishes energy basin, inhabited realm, starscape, dungeon, domai and political domain. MK727 `live-model/02_WORLD.md` already has realm and sprite summary. Do not overwrite AERA's modularity.
