# MK727 antagonist / geopolitical scope
Four HUMAN criminal/systemic antagonist categories, one distinct nonmoral environmental pressure. **Bandits:** decentralized roadside extortion, tolls, theft, caravan ambush, independent cells; present **Red through White**, tone can range comic to serious, never fully eliminated by dismantling one organization. **Cartels:** regional organized economic coercion, debt, markets, blackmail, geopolitical leverage; principal **Yellow, Green, Blue**; institutionally displaced/reduced at kingdom level after main conflicts, residual later appearances possible. **Pirates:** international maritime slave and illicit drug trade, mobile ocean hubs and fleets; principal **Green, Blue, Violet**, residual later involvement possible. **Cabal of Ash and Crown:** long-term covert magi institutional/sovereign capture, world domination; principal **Blue, Violet, White**. Organizations have independent objectives; do not assert single shared supreme command. Original Cabal and Pirate King are hostile in Genesis Point source. Adapt old magical methods to Genesis Card/AERA mechanics rather than importing incompatible magic.

**Environmental fifth pressure:** Genesis instability/collapse and Eldris; monsters are not moral villains. Book **White** ends with Kaira stabilizing planetary Genesis Field, ending collapses and collapse-driven monster emergence; exact fate of existing Eldris OPEN. Do not infer Cabal causes collapses.

**Geopolitical escalation:** Red/Orange personal, road and kingdom-adjacent life; Yellow cartels introduce kingdom/continental politics; Green cross-border cartel/pirate links; Blue/Violet global piracy and Cabal; White global human conflict and planetary environmental resolution. Kaira knows royal family, is princess's friend, king owns chainmail robe, but she is not early-book royal policymaker. Cartels may cross kingdom borders but are regional; pirate operations intercontinental; Cabal global. Example seven/eight cartels and three kingdoms on a continent is illustrative, not locked world map. Author-described planet ~80,000 miles **diameter**, 5-mile Genesis trees, mountains up to ~5× Kilimanjaro elevation; physics and map OPEN. Older Genesis Point pirate PDF's ~60,000-mile circumference is source-only and incompatible.
