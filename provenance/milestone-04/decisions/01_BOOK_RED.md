# Book Red narrative and continuity — M04
Six arcs remain I Village Hub, II Orange District, III Chainmail Robe Movement, IV Blackstone, V Aithren Community, VI King's Tournament. Long-form high fantasy: author aims ~900–1300 pages per book, substantial slice-of-life, character, relationships and worldbuilding; no compressed power-up ladder. Book colors are narrative labels, not realm or basin assignments.

Arc I: preserve Kaira age 23, mother Cardsmith died at 15, seven smithy years ages 16–23, forge sleeping and one evening meal, minimal wage and punitive deductions, three maternal technical books and mother's ordinary Fireball protection, fatigue after ~2–3 shots, manual chainmail, secret roadside harvesting during smith-owned donkey/cart deliveries. During localized collapse she recovers R–V and rare White crystals (used in exceptional restoration) plus altered flora and a living black mushroom `vaen`. She may sell some specimens, keeps mushroom and perhaps another living specimen as houseplants. Exact taxonomy, species, event choreography OPEN. The mushroom is eventually CONSUMED in first Black Card construction.

Arc II: independent city, abeca, Raeon, shopkeepers and Blacksmith Row; buys professionally manufactured prepared chainmail rings (20,000 is illustrative) from a local smith and assembles robe using proposed three-basin **General Metal Manipulation** card. Ring opening, orienting, interlocking and closing are small controlled material operations. She is expert manually but novice in Transduction control; she fumbles, improves. Her Fireball remains sentimental defense. Expanded Genesis Sight is an attractive early observational card, not a forced acquisition.

Arc III: chainmail robe fashion, commissions, independent income, social identity and growing professional card toolkit. King owns a robe as author-supplied background. Kaira has early maternal instruction (not full qualification), experiments with sigil workbench, modifying existing cards and engineering blank maege-glass cards; occasional costly fracture/failure. First minor successful original card is possible, not fixed. She collects living `vaen` as plants and has some post-restoration sensitivity to Genesis circulation; exact perceptual mechanism OPEN. Finesse and energy-field configuration control transfer partly from rings to Black Armor and later Genesis Orbs without guaranteeing mastery.

Arc IV Blackstone = **formal Cardsmith education**, mathematics, topology, differential geometry, material science, sigils and practical engineering; no requirement to construct Armor here. Supersedes earlier WORKING Armor-in-Arc-IV placement.

Arc V Aithren = voluntary dungeon-delving, friendships, small Cardsmithing commissions for community, team formation. **Armor of the Abyss is the first major original Black Card, engineered from blank maege glass during approximately first third to half of Arc V**, using the black mushroom as a special stabilizing vaen. Exact recipe OPEN. Remainder develops armor use, delving, combat competency and relationships.

Arc VI King's Tournament = team competition. **AUTHOR-LOCKED Yellow/fourth place**, no outright win. Kaira receives high-end Dimensional Ring plus monetary prize after competition; ring not available during competition or prior arcs. Ring is finite, recursive-topology conservation with conscious identity selection, not pocket dimension or inventory UI. Core traveling kit can include cards, mother's books, tools, chainmail stock, bedroll, provisions; compact portable Cardsmith table a future possibility only. After team achievement companions accept a professional `vaelum` offer; Kaira declines to pursue independent Cardsmithing/exploration; friendships continue, future reunion possible. Team names, ring tier, cash sum OPEN.

Books Orange–White: ring supports travel and realm `vaen` expeditions. Kaira's first Black Armor Red, CSR Orange, Orbs Yellow, Green development, Halo Blue, Domain Violet, integration White remain as inherited WORKING saga scaffolding. No mandated sixth Black card. Distinguish acquisition from mastery.
