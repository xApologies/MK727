# Civilization, nobility and Wilds — author directions
Civilization occupies stabilized islands in a vast Genesis-risk Wilds landscape. Wilds is not a biome: Genesis storms, collapse, Eldris, altered flora/fauna and rare vaen occur across ecosystems. Roads and guard waystations connect protected settlements; guard detachments patrol routes, often for months per circuit and years per assignment. Waystations allow lodging, repairs and resupply; organic towns can develop, but are not automatically Crown Village Hubs.
**City sigil array:** representative four corner sigil constructs and one central coordinating construct, all designed by Architects/Runesmiths, stabilize a large surrounding radius and reduce major collapses/domai, without eliminating all minor events or preventing existing Eldris from entering. 20-ft inscribed cube is illustrative. Small city: 7x7 miles and ~100-mile radius; large city: 10x10 and ~200-mile radius; capital: 10x10 and ~300-mile radius. These are working geographic specifications, not exact field equations.
**Small city districts:** Red outer, Orange middle, Yellow inner, White civic center. Three chromatic landed nobles own corresponding districts and surrounding rural stabilized land in Red 1/6, Orange 2/6, Yellow 3/6 shares. **Large/capital districts:** Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Violet inward, then White center. Six landed nobles own corresponding districts and surrounding rural territory in 1:2:3:4:5:6 shares (denominator 21). Rural shares exclude urban area and are not exact geometric wedges on a surveyed map; terrain/overlap/open water matter.
Chromatic nobles manage district sanitation, safety, infrastructure and property taxes and hold estates, orchards, vineyards, leased alchemical farmland, etc. White noble is a Crown-appointed civic overseer, without a separate landed rural share, supervising district nobles. Crown may replace neglectful White administrators; severe negligent chromatic nobles may be sanctioned or lose nobility. Red district is affordable and dense, not inherently dirty or crime-ridden. Civic duty is to maintain security, not guarantee literal zero crime. Tax rates and succession OPEN.
**Kingdom political map:** EXACTLY four royal domains: Violet (King and Queen at kingdom capital), Orange (Prince), Green (Prince), Blue (Princess); one capital per domain. Royal domains are distinct from city chromatic districts and Genesis energy basins. Original hand-drawn map determines borders and capital marker positions; do not invent an alternate map or kingdom name. Capitals have permanent transit gates. City location and Kaira's Arc II destination remain OPEN.
